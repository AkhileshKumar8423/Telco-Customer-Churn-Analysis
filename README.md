# 📊 TELCO CUSTOMER CHURN ANALYSIS

### Power BI | Customer Churn | Customer Retention | Customer Segmentation | Data Analytics

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**Telco Customer Churn Analysis** is an interactive Power BI dashboard project designed to analyze customer churn, retention patterns, customer behavior, services, contracts, and customer segments.

The project transforms customer-level data into meaningful business insights using **Power BI, DAX, data transformation, and interactive data visualization**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall customer churn and retention.
* Identify customer groups with higher churn rates.
* Understand the relationship between contracts and customer churn.
* Analyze customer tenure and customer behavior.
* Examine the impact of payment methods and subscribed services.
* Segment customers for deeper business analysis.
* Present key insights through interactive Power BI dashboards.

# 📈 Key Analysis Areas

### Customer Churn

Analysis of customers who have discontinued their services and identification of churn patterns.

### Customer Retention

Understanding customer retention behavior and identifying factors associated with continued service usage.

### Customer Segmentation

Grouping customers based on relevant characteristics and behavioral patterns.

### Contract Analysis

Analyzing how different contract types relate to customer churn and retention.

### Service Analysis

Examining subscribed services and their relationship with customer behavior.

### Payment Method Analysis

Comparing customer behavior across different payment methods.

### Tenure Analysis

Understanding how customer tenure relates to churn and retention.
